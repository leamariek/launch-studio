# Story Architectures

Pick one. Justify it against the audience ranking. Beat percentages are of total runtime.

## 1. The Shift (best for investors, category creation)
World changed → old tools can't keep up → a new approach is now possible → here it is, working → what opens up → line.
0–15% the shift · 15–35% the strain · 35–60% the product arrives · 60% turn · 60–90% proof montage · 90–100% line + CTA.
Use when "why now" is the strongest dossier answer.

## 2. Before / After (best for customers, workflow products)
A specific person's broken moment → the product in their hands → the same moment, transformed.
Mirror structure: the after-sequence rhymes visually with the before-sequence (same framing, same clock, different outcome).
Use when "what was broken" is vivid and felt.

## 3. The Origin (best for talent, press, founder-led brands)
The moment someone saw it → the obsession → what they built → who it's for now.
Needs a true, specific origin. Never fabricate one.

## 4. The Demo Is the Story (best for developer tools, highly visual products)
Cold open on the product doing the impossible thing → rewind: how → escalate: it does more → line.
Minimal copy; UI carries the argument. Supers are labels and punches.

## 5. The Manifesto (best for mission-driven, platform, rebrand)
A belief, stated plainly → evidence the belief is true → the product as proof of the belief → invitation.
Highest risk of sounding generic. Only use with a sharp, specific belief.

## Structural rules for all
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: motion, contrast, or a question. Never a logo.
- The turn lands at about 60%.
- The product name appears once in body copy at most; the logo lives at the end.
- End card holds for at least 2.5 s with the line and one CTA.
