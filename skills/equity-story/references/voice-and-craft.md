# Voice and Line Craft (US-native English)

## Principles
1. **Specific beats clever.** "Close the books in an afternoon" beats "Finance, reimagined."
2. **Verbs do the work.** Cut adjectives first, adverbs second.
3. **Say it like a person.** If a founder wouldn't say it across a table, don't put it on screen.
4. **Earn the big line.** Abstract lines work only after concrete beats have made them true.
5. **Rhythm.** Short. Short. Then a longer line that lands the thought. Then short again.

## Techniques
- **The concrete swap.** Replace every abstraction with the thing itself: "productivity" → "four hours back every Friday."
- **The contrast pair.** "Your data had answers. Now it has a voice." Use once per film, not every beat.
- **The unfinished line.** Let the UI finish the sentence: super reads "Ask it anything," UI types the question.
- **The callback.** The end line echoes a word from the opening beat.
- **Number discipline.** Numerals on screen ("3×", "40%"), with the unit. Round honestly. Never "up to" unless the source says so.

## Line testing
For each candidate end line, score 1–5 on: specific to this product, true, memorable after one hearing, sayable aloud, works as a headline on the landing page. Keep lines scoring 20+.

## Banned words and patterns
leverage, seamless(ly), revolutionize, unlock, empower, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-gen(eration), supercharge, elevate, robust, synergy, disrupt, "the future of X," "X, reimagined," "meet X" as an opener, "in today's fast-paced world," "whether you're A or B," "say goodbye to," "not just X, it's Y," "powered by AI" as a benefit.

## US English conventions
Sentence case for on-screen lines unless the brand uses title case. Oxford comma, unless the product's own copy leaves it out (then follow the product). "Sign up" (verb) vs. "signup" (noun). Dates as "March 12." Currency as "$4M."

## Before / after examples
- ✗ "Leverage AI to seamlessly streamline your workflow." ✓ "Drop in the contract. Get the redlines back before lunch."
- ✗ "The future of design collaboration." ✓ "Every comment, in context. Every version, one click back."
- ✗ "Unlock insights from your data." ✓ "Ask your data a question. Get an answer, not a dashboard."
